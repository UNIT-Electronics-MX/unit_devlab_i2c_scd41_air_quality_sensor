## **Description**

The DevLab I2C SCD41 Air Quality Sensor (MPN **UE0179**) is an I2C air quality sensing module in the UNIT Electronics DevLab family. The product-specific board views and any released drawings are collected under `hardware/`.

### **Applications**

- Air quality sensing in an I2C-based prototype.
- Evaluation of the module with a compatible host controller.

### **Available Documentation**

The UE0179 board schematic and pinout appear under `hardware/`. Chapter 8 lists the source files, and Appendix 9.4 shows the complete schematic.
