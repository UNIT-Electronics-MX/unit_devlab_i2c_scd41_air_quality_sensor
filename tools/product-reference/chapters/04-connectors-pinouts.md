### **4.2 Connection Groups** {.page-break}

| Group | Labels in the released drawing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Qwiic I2C connector | `GND`, `VCC`, `SDA`, `SCL` | Power and I2C bus connections |
| Board contacts | `VCC`, `GND`, `SCL`, `SDA` | Direct access to the labeled power and bus signals |

The drawing also marks optional 4-pin, 1 mm JST connector positions. The
pinout does not establish electrical limits or connector part numbers.
